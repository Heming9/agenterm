Product name
Agenterm

Tagline
Let your AI agent help on another computer

Description
Agenterm connects your existing AI agent to remote terminals through MCP. Troubleshoot, inspect logs, and work across devices with explicit authorization. See commands and output, disconnect anytime. Open source, self-hostable, and no SSH required.

Short launch post
Hi Product Hunt! I built Agenterm to let the AI agent you already use help on another computer.

Connect your agent via MCP and privately share an invitation link or connection command. The person at the target computer authorizes access in Agenterm. Your agent can then troubleshoot, inspect logs, or work across dev machines and servers through the terminal. The person who authorized access can see commands and output and disconnect at any time.

Agenterm is open source and self-hostable. Its Rust terminal client runs on macOS, Linux, and Windows, using an outbound connection from the target computer.

Try it: https://agenterm.net
